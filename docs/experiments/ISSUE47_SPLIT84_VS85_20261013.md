# Issue #47: GQA split-count 84 versus 85 — RTX 5080

**Experiment date:** 2026-10-13
**Status:** Completed — no production promotion recommended
**Parent:** [NInfer Issue #47](https://github.com/toddballinger/ninfer-5080/issues/47)

## Objective

Investigate whether reducing the 27B GQA decode split count
from 85 to 84 improves performance on an 84-SM RTX 5080.

This experiment concerns the split-count hypothesis only.
It does not resolve the separate Q4-tiled spill/scheduling
investigation or the proposed 64/52/42 split variants.

## Benchmark results

All latency values are microseconds.

| Metric | 85 splits | 84 splits | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T=1 median | 303.104 | 302.272 | -0.832 (-0.274%) |
| T=4 median | 295.424 | 294.944 | -0.480 (-0.162%) |
| T=1 workspace bytes | 1,060,832 | 1,048,448 | -12,384 |
| T=4 workspace bytes | 4,243,328 | 4,193,280 | -50,048 |

These are single sequential 85-then-84 measurements.
The observed differences are too small to establish
a repeatable or material performance improvement.

## Decision

**Retain 85 splits in production.**

The experimental 84-split route passed the targeted
correctness tests, but the observed improvement does
not justify production integration, additional complexity
or another maintenance window for this variation.

Preserve this branch as reproducible negative-result
evidence. Do not merge its experimental launcher into
main without a separate implementation and qualification
decision.
